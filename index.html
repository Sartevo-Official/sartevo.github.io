FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

How much does it cost?

There is no standard price. After the consultation you receive a written proposal with the scope and cost, made up of a one-time setup fee and a monthly subscription for maintenance and support.

Are third-party software costs included in the pricing?

No. Third-party software subscriptions, platform fees, API usage charges, and external service costs are billed separately when applicable.

Do I need technical knowledge to use the system?

No. We handle the setup, configuration, and implementation process. Your team only needs to use the system as part of their daily workflow.

Can the system be customized for my business?

Yes. Each business operates differently. We design and configure systems based on your specific processes, goals, and operational requirements.

How long does implementation take?

Implementation timelines vary depending on project scope and complexity. A timeframe is provided after the discovery and scoping process.

What happens after the system is delivered?

When the system is delivered, we walk your team through how to use it day to day. After launch, the monthly subscription covers technical support, system monitoring, and bug fixes, so the system keeps working as intended.

Will I own my software accounts and subscriptions?

Yes. Clients maintain ownership of their software accounts, subscriptions, and related assets whenever applicable.

Can I add more automation later?

Yes. As your business grows, your system can be extended with additional automation, integrations, and services.
