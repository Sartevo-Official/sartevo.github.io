FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do I need technical knowledge to use the system?

No. We handle the setup, configuration, and implementation process. Your team only needs to use the system as part of their daily workflow.

Are third-party software costs included in the pricing?

No. Third-party software subscriptions, platform fees, API usage charges, and external service costs are billed separately when applicable.

Can the system be customized for my business?

Yes. Each business operates differently. We design and configure revenue systems based on your specific processes, goals, and operational requirements.

Will I own my software accounts and subscriptions?

Yes. Clients maintain ownership of their software accounts, subscriptions, and related assets whenever applicable.

What happens after the implementation?

We provide ongoing support and training to ensure your team is fully equipped to utilize the system effectively.

How long does implementation take?

Implementation timelines vary depending on project scope and complexity. A timeframe is provided after the discovery and scoping process.

How do I know which package is right for my business?

We begin with a consultation to understand your requirements before recommending the most appropriate solution.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. The monthly subscription includes ongoing technical support, system monitoring, and bug fixes to ensure your revenue system continues to operate effectively after launch.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Yes. As your business grows, your revenue system can be upgraded with additional automation, integrations, and services for increased capacity.
